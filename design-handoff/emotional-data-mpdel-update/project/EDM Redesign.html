§1Motivation

Today, affective context lives inside the model. That makes it non-portable, non-inspectable, and vulnerable to drift.

Contemporary LLMs internalise emotional, narrative, and motivational cues transiently within latent activations. Those signals don't survive model updates, vendor migrations, or cross-context transport — and they can't be audited. EDM treats this as a representational deficit, not a tuning problem.

§1.1 · DEFICIT

Representational

Emotional state lives as a transient byproduct of inference — not as structured data. It cannot be inspected, compared, or preserved.

§1.2 · GAP

Sovereignty

Governance — jurisdiction, consent, retention — is applied by platforms, not attached to the artifact. Rights evaporate on export.

§1.3 · PRINCIPLE

Representation before computation

Structure is defined first, independent of any inference engine. Compute reads; it does not redefine.

§2Architecture

Three layers, one invariant: representation precedes computation.

EDM separates what something is (Representation), how it is preserved (Persistence), and what can be done with it (Computation). The boundaries are enforced structurally, not by policy.

LAYER 03Computation

External compute — retrieval, appraisal, agentic workflows

Any downstream system reads from the persistence layer. EDM takes no position on the compute model, only on its source of truth.

§3Affective Manifold

Five core domains, ten total. Each with canonical keys, enumerations, and invariants.

The manifold is domain-complete: every EDM artifact populates the same structural surface, with no omissions and no inference. Governance domains extend this surface without mutating it.

§4.3.1 · CORE

Narrative Primitives

core

Identity, role, and narrative anchors. The discrete units on which higher-order affect composes.

§4.3.2 · TOPOLOGY

Emotional Topology

constellation

Relational geometry between affective states. The shape, not the intensity, of emotional configuration.

§4.3.3 · FRAMING

Contextual Framing

milky_way

Situational, temporal, and social framing under which affect is valid. Without frame, signal is noise.

§4.3.4 · SALIENCE

Salience & Dynamics

gravity

Weight, decay, and pull of each anchor over time. The dynamic field governing what persists and what fades.

§4.3.5 · ORIENTATION

Motivational Orientation

impulse

Direction of intent. What the subject is oriented toward or away from, independent of behavioural expression.

§5.ddna Envelope

Representation is preserved by a byte-stable, governance-bound envelope.

The .ddna envelope binds governance logic — jurisdiction, identity rights, retention — directly to the payload. Compliance persists during transport; it is not reconstructed at the destination.

.ddna · application/ddna+cbor

Sovereignty bound at the payload

  • CanonicalByte-stable serialisation. Two conformant encoders produce identical bytes for identical artifacts.
  • IntegrityCryptographic seal. SHA-256 + ed25519. Tampering is detectable without interpretation.
  • IdentityVitaPass / AuraID. Subject identity bound in-band, independent of platform.
  • JurisdictionalIn-band governance. Jurisdiction, consent, retention travel with the artifact.
  • StatelessReduced-governance mode available for interchange without subject identity.
